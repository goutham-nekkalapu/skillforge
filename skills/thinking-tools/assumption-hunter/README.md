# assumption-hunter

Surfaces hidden assumptions in arguments, plans, proposals, or writing—factual, causal, value-based, structural, and temporal. It maps what is being taken for granted, rates risk and testability, and recommends how to validate or harden the idea before you commit, without treating critique as combat.

## Platform

- **claude.ai**
- **Claude Code**
- **Cursor**
- Adaptable to other agentic frameworks

## Trigger Phrases

```
"What am I missing?"
"Poke holes in this"
"What are the risks?"
"Challenge my thinking"
"What assumptions am I making?"
"Is this solid?"
"Find my blind spots"
"What am I taking for granted?"
```
