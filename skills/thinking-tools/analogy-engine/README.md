# analogy-engine

Generates precise, non-clichéd analogies for complex ideas so abstract concepts feel concrete and unfamiliar topics click. It maps the structure of what you are explaining onto fresh domains, stress-tests where each analogy holds and breaks, and recommends the best fit for your audience—whether you are teaching, presenting, or explaining technical material in simple terms.

## Platform

- **claude.ai**
- **Claude Code**
- **Cursor**
- Adaptable to other agentic frameworks

## Trigger Phrases

```
"Give me an analogy for this"
"How do I explain this to someone?"
"What's this like?"
"ELI5 but with a good analogy"
"Help me make this concept click"
"I need a metaphor for this"
"What's a good analogy?"
"Make this relatable"
```
